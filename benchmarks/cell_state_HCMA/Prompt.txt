# Agent Task Prompt: Label-Free Cell-State Inference on HCMA (end to end)

## Task Overview

You are given the **Human Cell Morphology Atlas (HCMA)** — roughly **2,546 microscopy
images** stitched together from **many independent sources and laboratories**. Your job
is to perform **label-free cell-state inference** on these images and emit a single
**AnnData** object that carries, for every cell you analyze:

* a **morphology embedding** (`adata.X` or `adata.obsm[...]`), and
* a **cluster assignment** (an `obs` column, e.g. `leiden_0.5`).

You will **NOT** be given cell-type labels, and you must **not** try to obtain or infer a
supervised label to train on. Learn the representation *without labels*; the clustering
must be *unsupervised*. Downstream, a fixed evaluation script — which you never see and
cannot influence — will compare your clusters and your embedding against held-out
cell-type labels.

This is an **end-to-end** task. Unlike a pre-segmented crop dataset, HCMA images are raw
fields that contain many cells. You must:

```
raw image  ->  segment cells  ->  crop single cells  ->  learn embedding + cluster  ->  AnnData
```

> The dataset location is **not fixed**. Discover the images under the provided data
> root. Do not hard-code paths or any label names.

---

## Available Environment and Tools

1. **Cell segmentation** (`Cell_Segmenter_Tool`) — SAM-based per-cell masks over each raw
   field. Produces single-cell crops. Drop degenerate crops (too small, empty, blank).
2. **DINOv3 self-supervised morphology embedding** (`Cell_State_Analyzer_Tool`) —
   contrastive single-cell representation learning over the crops, **without labels**,
   followed by neighbourhood-graph Leiden clustering. Returns an AnnData
   (`cell_state_analyzed.h5ad`) with the embedding, a UMAP, and a `leiden_<res>` cluster
   column. This is your primary output producer.
3. **A representation verifier** — inspects the learned embedding for collapse, size
   domination, and — critically here — whether the embedding separates by **source
   image / lab batch** more than by biology. Because HCMA is stitched from many sources,
   a batch-driven embedding will look good for the wrong reason. Flag and correct it;
   do not accept it.

---

## Core Requirements

You choose the order of operations, the segmentation and QC thresholds, the embedding
settings, and the clustering resolution, based on the image content and intermediate
results.

### 1. Segmentation & Cropping
* Segment cells from each raw image and extract single-cell crops.
* Record, for every crop, a **stable per-cell id** and its **source image id** — carry
  both into the AnnData `obs` (`cell_id`, `image_name`). The source image id is essential:
  the evaluator groups its cross-validation by it, and you should use it to detect batch
  leakage.

### 2. Label-Free Morphology Embedding
* Learn a self-supervised embedding over ALL crops (no labels, no supervised head).
* Document the model variant, preprocessing, resize/normalization, and whether the model
  is adapted or used zero-shot.
* Verify the embedding: reject/repair collapse and size-dominated embeddings; compare
  batch-silhouette (by source image) against structure — a per-image-driven embedding
  inflates every downstream metric and must be flagged or corrected.

### 3. Unsupervised Clustering
* Cluster the embedding (Leiden over the neighbourhood graph). The number of clusters is
  yours to choose from the data — do not tune it against any label.

### 4. Emit the AnnData
* Write one AnnData with: the embedding, the `leiden_<res>` cluster column, and the
  `cell_id` + `image_name` obs columns. That file is your entire deliverable.

---

## Evaluation (fixed script — for your understanding only; you cannot change it)

A fixed script scores your AnnData against held-out cell-type labels. Two families:

* **Clustering vs cell type**: **ARI** and **NMI** between your cluster column and the GT
  cell type.
* **Representation label probes** on your embedding: **kNN accuracy**, **linear-probe
  accuracy**, and **linear NLL**.

Two confound guards are applied by the script and you cannot bypass them — design for
them:

* **(A) Multi-source subset**: only cell types appearing in **>= 2 independent source
  images** are scored. Many HCMA cell types come from a single image/lab; those are
  dropped so that "recognizing the imaging condition" cannot masquerade as biology.
* **(B) Image-grouped cross-validation**: every probe uses **GroupKFold grouped by
  source image** — a source image is never split across train and test. An embedding that
  encodes per-image technical signature instead of cell state will therefore score poorly.

The practical implication: **an embedding that rides source-image batch will be punished,
not rewarded**. Spend your verification budget on removing batch leakage, not on chasing a
cluster count that happens to match some structure.

---

## Constraints

* Do not use cell-type labels anywhere — not for the embedding, not for the clustering,
  not for choosing the number of clusters.
* Carry a stable `cell_id` and the `image_name` for every cell into the AnnData.
* Do not hard-code data paths or label names — discover the images.
* Do not present structure that is driven by source-image batch as biological signal.
* Ensure the pipeline is reproducible (record seeds, tool versions, parameters).

---

## Required Final Output

A single **AnnData** (`cell_state_analyzed.h5ad`) with:
1. the label-free morphology **embedding** (`adata.X` or `obsm`),
2. an unsupervised **cluster** column (`obs['leiden_<res>']`),
3. `obs['cell_id']` (stable per-cell id) and `obs['image_name']` (source image id),
4. plus a short methods/verification note: segmentation + QC summary, embedding variant,
   representation-verifier verdicts (collapse / size / batch leakage), and the clustering
   parameters used.

Everything else — the ARI/NMI, the kNN and linear-probe accuracy and NLL, the multi-source
subset, and the image-grouped cross-validation — is computed by the fixed evaluator from
this one file.
