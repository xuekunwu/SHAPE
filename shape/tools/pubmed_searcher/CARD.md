# Tool card: Pubmed_Searcher_Tool

**When to use:** retrieve biomedical literature (article abstracts) from PubMed Central for a
list of text queries -- literature mining, marker/gene grounding, functional evidence.
**When NOT:** not an image tool; outside the bioimage pipeline. Input: text queries. Output:
matching article abstracts/metadata. Also used internally by
`Cell_Cluster_Functional_Hypothesis_Tool`.
