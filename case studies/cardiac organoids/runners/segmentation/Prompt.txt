# Cardiac organoid instance segmentation

## Target
Segment every whole cardiac organoid or spheroid as a separate instance, including
objects touching the image border. Outline its full visible outer boundary.
Internal cells, lumens, texture and halos are not separate objects. Do not include
debris or background. Touching distinct organoids must have different instance IDs.

## Deliverable
Deliver a 2D integer instance mask at the original image size: background 0,
one unique positive ID per organoid. The task ends at segmentation.

For tool-assisted segmentation use Organoid_Segmenter_Tool with its fixed custom
shape_seg_co weights. Choose and deliver a completed segmentation result.
No downstream cropping, representation learning or biological classification.
